# 5.3 scope: Gramatické číslo (singulár vs. plurál)

Written by P18 W2-53 (retroactive scope-rule rework, text and audio only, pictures unchanged). The decision is logged in the task's open_questions.md under [P18-W2].

## Type
- Name: 5.3 Gramatické číslo. The child matches a sentence to a picture showing one or several referents (TreninkPorozumeni doc 5.3.1; the original doc wording is "bez pomoci číslovek").
- Core contrast: one vs. several of the same referent. The grammatical distractor is the same scene with the other number.

## Forms inventory
- Number on the subject noun, with verb agreement: „Psi běží po louce." / „Pes běží po louce." Depictable: yes. This is the current form of most items.
- Number on the object or another non-subject noun; the verb gives no cue: „Kluk krmí psy." / „Kluk krmí psa." Depictable: yes. Already 18 items, both variants.
- Indefinite quantifier „několik" + genitive plural, with the verb in the singular: „Po louce běží několik psů." Depictable: yes, for 3–4 referents. ÚJČ: with několik/pět the predicate agrees in the singular (neuter -o in the past).
- Cardinal numeral 2–4 + nominative plural: „Dvě žirafy jedí listy." Depictable: yes, only when the picture count matches exactly.
- Numeral 5+ + genitive plural + singular verb („Pět psů běží"). Depictable: no; the pictures show only 2–4 referents.
- Irregular or suppletive plurals (dítě/děti, kotě/koťata, člověk/lidé, oko/oči): depictable yes, but only where pictures exist. Dítě, kotě, kuře and štěně items exist already; they are counted as majority because number is still marked on the subject noun.
- Number marked only by the verb, with an elided or pronoun subject („Běží po louce."): risky. „Běží" is the same form in sg and pl, so the sentence is ambiguous. Excluded.
- Nouns with identical sg/pl forms (ovce, růže) or pluralia tantum (dveře, nůžky): no existing pictures. Excluded.

## Majority (marked) form
Bare number on the subject noun in the nominative, with the verb agreeing (sg or pl), and no numeral.

## Minority form(s)
1. Number on the object or another non-subject noun (existing 18 items, both variants). The verb is identical in both variants, so the child must decode the noun ending (psa/psy, kost/kosti).
2. Number with a quantifier or numeral. „několik + 2nd case plural" takes a singular verb (for 3–4 referents). „dva/dvě/tři/čtyři + noun" is used where the picture count matches exactly. These forms are common in normal speech. The child must not rely on the singular verb („běží několik psů" still means many).

Why: the original doc says „bez pomoci číslovek". _TestTypeScopeRule.md §7 overrides it for a minority of about 1/3, which keeps the bare-number majority.

## Contrast kept by minority items
The grammatical distractor is always the picture with the other number (1 vs. 2–4). Lexical pictures stay wrong (another referent or another action).
